
# 📊 PeoplePulse | HR Workforce & Attrition Analytics

An end-to-end HR analytics project analyzing voluntary employee turnover across a workforce of 16,000 employees. This project focuses on identifying turnover drivers, examining departmental disparities, evaluating compensation impacts, and proposing actionable retention strategies.

![Dashboard Preview](powerbi%20hr.jpg)
---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Domain:** Human Resources (HR) Analytics
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel, Power BI Desktop, DAX
* **Dataset Scope:** 16,000 employee records across 9 departments

---

## 🔍 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Workforce:** 16,000
* **Total Leavers:** 1,832
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 11.4%
* **Average Monthly Salary:** 13,640

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Analytical Insights

1. **Overtime & Burnout:**
   * Leavers worked higher average overtime (**10.1 hrs/month**) compared to active employees (**9.2 hrs/month**), underscoring operational burnout as a key exit driver.

2. **Departmental Vulnerabilities:**
   * Highest voluntary turnover was identified in commercial departments:
     * **Marketing:** 13.2%
     * **Sales:** 12.6%
     * **IT:** 12.2%
   * **Logistics** recorded the lowest attrition rate at **9.2%**.

3. **Demographic Dynamics:**
   * Mid-career professionals aged **30–39** formed the largest group of leavers at **27.18%** (498 employees), followed by the **50+** segment at **26.15%**.

4. **Compensation Breakdown:**
   * While the **Low Salary** tier had the highest relative attrition rate (**11.9%**), the **Medium Salary** bracket constituted over **55% of all leavers** (1,008 employees), creating the highest replacement overhead.

5. **Promotion Lag:**
   * Employees without a promotion in the last 2 years had an attrition rate of **12.0%**, compared to **9.5%** for promoted staff.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Business Recommendations
* **Target Commercial Retention:** Re-evaluate quota structures and work-life balance policies within Sales and Marketing.
* **Cap Overtime:** Institute an upper threshold on monthly overtime to mitigate employee burnout.
* **Mid-Career Growth Tracks:** Build structured leadership pathways for employees aged 30–39.
* **Streamline Promotion Cycles:** Ensure transparent advancement timelines that do not exceed 24 months for top contributors.
